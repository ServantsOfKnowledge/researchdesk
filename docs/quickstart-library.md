# Quick start: running the library

For the library manager: set the library up, decide who can do what, and see how it is going.

## Set it up (about half an hour)

1. **Follow the checklist.** Research Desk's opening page has a getting-started checklist: name and logo, a first selection of books, watching the ingest, access, a first collection, notes and proofreading. Each step opens the right screen. [More](staff-guide.md#the-getting-started-checklist)
2. **Name it.** Desk → **Settings**: the portal's name, logo and the kind of library you are. [More](staff-guide.md#settings)
3. **Choose what it does.** Settings → **Features**: tick the kinds of institution you are (a school, a university, an archive...) and only the features you need switch on. Switched-off features are not collected. [More](staff-guide.md#features-and-your-institution)
4. **Decide who can read what.** Public, members only, or a mix. [More](access.md)
5. **Set up sign-in email** if readers will sign up. [More](sign-in-email.md)

## People

Desk → **People & Roles**: a card per role, tick boxes to give or take a role, invite people by email, approve sign-ups, switch accounts off. Give **ResDesk Cataloguer** to people who catalogue and lend, **ResDesk Proofreader** to volunteers who correct pages. [More](staff-guide.md#people-and-roles)

## Each week

- Look at the dashboard on the Research Desk page: books, readers, searchable pages, preservation.
- Read **Messages** from visitors. [More](staff-guide.md#messages-from-visitors)
- Open **Portal Statistics** to see how the portal is used (if you switched usage statistics on). [More](staff-guide.md#portal-statistics)
- Look at the **Server** page once in a while: health, backups and new releases. [More](server.md)
