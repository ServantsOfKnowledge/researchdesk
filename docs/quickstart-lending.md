# Quick start: the lending desk

For the people who lend and take back books. If lending has not been switched on yet, a manager does that first ([turning it on](lending.md#turning-it-on)).

## At the desk

Open Desk → **Circulation Desk**. A barcode scanner types the code and presses Enter, so every box works with it.

1. **Lend.** Scan the member's card, then scan each book. The due date comes from the member's category and the days the library is closed.
2. **Take back.** Scan the book. A late fine is charged by the category's rule. If someone is waiting for it, the screen says to keep it on the hold shelf.
3. **Renew.** Press **Renew** on the loan (not allowed while someone is waiting for the book).
4. **Lost.** Press **Lost**: the loan closes and the copy is marked lost.
5. **Fines.** Mark a fine **Paid**, or **Waive** it.

A refusal always says why: member expired or blocked, too many loans, fines over the limit, copy already out, lost, reference only, or kept for someone else. [Everything about the desk](lending.md#the-circulation-desk)

## Members and copies

- **A new member:** Desk → **Members** → New: name, category, the number on their ID card and a photo. Save: the member number on their library card is made for you. Print the card from the member. [More](lending.md#members)
- **A new book copy:** Desk → **Copies** → New (or receive an order): the barcode is made when you save. **Print Barcode Label** for the sticker. [More](lending.md#copies)
- **Holds:** when every copy is out, a member can place a hold on the book's page. The next returned copy goes on the hold shelf. [More](lending.md#holds)

## Each day

- Look at the two lists on the Circulation Desk: what is **overdue**, and what waits on the **hold shelf**.
- No email goes out about loans. Members see what is due on **My library**.
