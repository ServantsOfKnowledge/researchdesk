# Signing in, your account and About me

This page is for readers. If something here does not match what you see, ask the library: it may
have set things up differently.

## Creating an account

On the portal, choose **Log in**, then **Create an account** (if the library offers it). Give your
name and your email address. Depending on the library:

- **Anyone can sign up**: you get an email to set your password, then you can read at once.
- **Sign up, and a librarian approves**: you get the email too, but until you are approved you see
  what visitors see. The library lets you know when you are in.
- **The library adds readers**: there is no sign-up link. Ask the library for an account.

## The email does not arrive

1. Look in the spam or junk folder, and wait a few minutes.
2. Check that you typed the address correctly.
3. Try **Forgot password?** on the login page: it sends a new link.
4. Still nothing? Tell the library. A librarian can see whether the library's email is set up and
   can create the account for you. See [Sign-in email and sign-ups](sign-in-email.md).

## About me (all optional)

Open **About me** from the Account menu, or at `/library/profile`. Tell the library as much or as
little as you like.

- **Who you are**: a person or an organisation, your role or interest, place, website and a few
  words. Tick *The library may name me as a contributor* only if you want to be named.
- **Would you like to help?** Tick that you would like to **review** deposits and catalogue records,
  or to **proofread** page text, and say what languages, subjects or time you can offer. A librarian
  sees this and can give you the right access.
- **Support (private)**: say if you are blind, have low vision, are deaf or hard of hearing, have a
  motor difficulty or dyslexia, or something else, and what would help (large text, a screen-reader
  walk-through, audio, more time). Only you and the library's managers see it. It is never shown
  publicly.

You can change or empty it at any time.

## Making the portal easier to read

The **Aa** button at the top of every page changes text size, spacing and colours. See
[Accessibility](accessibility.md) for everything that helps, and how to tell the library what does
not work for you.
