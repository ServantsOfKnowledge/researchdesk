# Connections: everything Research Desk shares with other systems

**Research Desk → Exchange → Connections** lists every outside system the library works with, by
kind. For each one it says what it does, whether it is on or connected *for you*, who may use it,
and gives the buttons that open the right screen. Nothing is hidden: a connection your role does not
use is shown greyed out, so you know it exists and whom to ask.

## The kinds

| Kind | What is in it |
|---|---|
| **Internet Archive** | bring books in from archive.org; give a book to archive.org under your own account (always public); keep archive.org's details in step |
| **Wikimedia** | your own Wikimedia account; Wikidata and authority control; Wikisource in and out; Wikimedia Commons for photographs |
| **Library systems** | Koha, Evergreen, SOUL or any MARC catalogue matched to the books here, links sent back |
| **Repositories and deposit** | DSpace, EPrints and OAI-PMH repositories in; people's own deposited work, reviewed; ARKs and DOIs |
| **E-books and offline** | Calibre in and out; offline copies for Kiwix; the OPDS catalogue for e-reader apps |
| **Open standards for others to reach this library** | OAI-PMH, SRU, IIIF, the COUNTER report, with each address to copy |
| **Import and export** | spreadsheet and metadata-file imports; every export format; moving or backing up the library |
| **Webhooks and your own tools** | signed notifications to your own systems |
| **People and sign-in** | **Outgoing email**: the mailbox sign-in, sign-up and password emails are sent from, with a test and the failed emails; **Reader profiles**: what readers chose to tell the library, and who offers to help ([Sign-in email and sign-ups](sign-in-email.md)) |

## What the chips mean

- **Green**: on, or connected as you ("Connected as …").
- **Amber**: something waits for you: connect your account, deposits to review, matches to review.
- **Blue**: on, with a count (for example "2 library systems").
- **Grey**: the feature is switched off in Settings → Features; a manager can switch it on.

## Who sees what

Staff (managers, cataloguers, proofreaders) open the page. Each card names who may use it, and its
buttons are disabled when your role does not. Depositors, who work on the portal and not in the
Desk, find their archive.org connection and the send button on the deposit page. The accounts and
keys behind each connection (archive.org, Wikimedia, DataCite) belong to the person who connected
them, or to the super admin for the installation's own credentials; they are never shown.
