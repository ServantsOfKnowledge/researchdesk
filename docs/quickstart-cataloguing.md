# Quick start: cataloguing

For cataloguers: bring books in, keep their records right, and gather them into collections. Each step names the screen and where to read more.

## Your first hour

1. **Bring books in.** Desk → **Ingest Profiles** → open a profile (two samples come with the install) or make one: choose by collection, search, list of identifiers or your own folders. Press **Check Count**, then **Run Ingest**. Start small (50 to 500 books). [More](ingesting.md#ingest-profiles)
2. **Watch it run.** **Run Ingest** opens the progress page; **Background Jobs** shows every run and can pause one. Books arrive in the background, details first, page text after. [More](operations.md#background-jobs-see-pause-and-stop-what-is-running)
3. **Look at the books.** Desk → **Items**. Open one to see its record; correct the title, year or language and **Save**. Your edits are kept through later ingests.
4. **Clear the review queue.** Desk → **Review Queue** lists the records that most likely need a person's eye, worst first. Correct them on the page, or press **This is right** when a record really is that way. [More](staff-guide.md#the-review-queue)
5. **Gather books into a collection.** Desk → **Collections**: give it a name and a picture and add books, so readers can browse them together. [More](staff-guide.md#collections)

## Every day

- Check the **Review Queue** for new questions (it is checked again each night).
- Fix authors and subjects once, in **Authorities**, instead of book by book. [More](staff-guide.md#authors-and-subjects)
- Answer **Deposits** that people have sent in: accept, ask for changes or decline. [More](deposit.md)
- Many books to correct at once? Export a spreadsheet, edit it and import it back. [More](collections-and-metadata.md)

## Other kinds of material

Books in folders on a disk or server ([local folders](local-folders.md)), photographs ([photographs](photographs.md)), manuscripts and palm leaves ([manuscripts](manuscripts.md)), recordings ([audio and video](audio-video.md)), archives from fonds to item ([archival description](archival-description.md)).

## When something looks wrong

- A book that will not come in: open the run's page; it says why. [Ingesting](ingesting.md)
- A page of text that is wrong: readers and proofreaders fix it in the reader. [Proofreading](staff-guide.md#proofreading-and-re-ocr)
- Anything else: the **Help** button on every screen opens the section for that screen.
